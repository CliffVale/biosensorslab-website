Aptamers are "chemical antibodies" with great potential wherever antibodies are used. Traditional aptamer selection is time-consuming and labor-intensive. Our lab developed a totally new concept — Hydrogel for Aptamer Selection (HAS) — using a non-fouling porous hydrogel that selects high-affinity aptamers in a single step in just 60 hours. The work was published in *Nature Biotechnology*.
