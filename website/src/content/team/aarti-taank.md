Aarti Taank is pursuing her Ph.D. at the Centre for Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. She completed her M.Sc. in Medical Biotechnology (Gold Medalist) and B.Sc. in Botany, Chemistry, and Zoology from Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak.

Her research focuses on wearable patches for chronic wound management. She has been awarded the prestigious CSIR Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) with an All India Rank of 98.
