---
title: "Point-of-Care SARS-CoV-2 Salivary Antigen Test Using a Glucometer"
date: 2021-05-01
summary: "An aptamer-based SARS-CoV-2 salivary antigen assay using only low-cost reagents ($3.20/test) and an off-the-shelf glucometer was published in Biosensors and Bioelectronics."
---
We reported an aptamer-based SARS-CoV-2 salivary antigen assay employing only low-cost reagents ($3.20/test) and an off-the-shelf glucometer. Because the glucometer is the most prevalent diagnostic device globally, the test is highly scalable with infrastructure that is already in place.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bios.2021.113111)
