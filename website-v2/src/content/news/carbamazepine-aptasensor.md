---
title: "Electrochemical Carbamazepine Aptasensor for Therapeutic Drug Monitoring"
date: 2022-11-01
summary: "Our electrochemical aptasensor for the antiepileptic drug carbamazepine was published in ACS Omega."
---
We developed an electrochemical aptasensor for carbamazepine — an antiepileptic drug that requires therapeutic drug monitoring — enabling sensitive detection of the drug in clinical samples.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.2c04865)
