---
title: "Non-Invasive Wearable Stress Patch for Real-Time Cortisol Monitoring"
date: 2023-05-01
summary: "Our wearable patch for continuous, non-invasive cortisol monitoring in sweat was published in Biosensors and Bioelectronics, with validation on human subjects under induced stress."
---
We reported a wearable patch based on soft microfluidics and flexible electrodes functionalized with a pseudoknot-assisted conformation-switching aptamer for pH-calibrated, continuous cortisol monitoring in sweat. The sensor exhibited a broad dynamic range (1 pM – 10 µM) with a sub-picomolar limit of detection, validated in real time on three human subjects with induced stress.
