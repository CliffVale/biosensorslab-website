Sutapa Ghosh is pursuing her M.Tech in Biomedical Engineering at IIT Delhi, specializing in biosensors and biomedical devices. She is a recipient of the DAAD-KOSPIE Fellowship, through which she conducted her master's project at RWTH Aachen University, Germany, focusing on biosensor surface optimization and materials for point-of-care diagnostics.

She holds a Master's degree in Biophysics and a Bachelor's degree in Physics, giving her a strong interdisciplinary foundation bridging physics, biology, and engineering. Previously, she worked as a Junior Research Fellow at the National Institute of Biomedical Genomics, contributing to pancreatic cancer research.
