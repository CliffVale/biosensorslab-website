Priyanshi Gupta is a Ph.D. scholar at the Centre for Biomedical Engineering (CBME), Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. She holds a Master's degree from Amity University, Noida, and gained valuable research experience at CSIR-National Physical Laboratory (NPL).

Her research interests lie in the field of biosensors, with a focus on advancing innovative diagnostic technologies.
