## Join Us

We are always looking for motivated students and researchers with backgrounds in chemistry, biology, electronics, or bioengineering. If you are interested in biosensors, point-of-care diagnostics, wearable devices, or programmable biomaterials, please get in touch!

## PhD Students

We are looking for PhD students with a strong background in any of: chemistry, biology, electronics, or bioengineering.

- Curiosity-driven research mindset
- Hands-on experimental skills (wet-lab or instrumentation)
- Interest in biosensors & point-of-care diagnostics

## Master Students

Motivated master students who want to dive deep into biosensor research are welcome.

- Passion for research
- Basic wet-lab or electronics skills
- Commitment to projects

## Undergraduates

Talented undergraduates can join us for research training, projects, and internships.

- Curiosity driven
- Fast learner
- Team player
