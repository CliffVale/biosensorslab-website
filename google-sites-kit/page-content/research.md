## Research Areas

Our lab explores the frontiers of artificial intelligence, with a focus on computer vision and machine learning.

##  UroSmart — Non-Invasive Aptasensor Platform for Pancreatic Cancer Detection

A fully integrated platform combining aseptic urine collection, automated microfluidic dispensing, and aptamer-based electrochemical sensing for point-of-care detection of CA 19.9 — the only clinically approved biomarker for pancreatic cancer.  [/research/urosmart]  Learn more

##  Instrument-Free Malaria Detection

Detection of pan-malaria and Plasmodium falciparum using dye-based reactions catalyzed by the biomarker enzymes Plasmodium lactate dehydrogenase (pLDH) and Plasmodium falciparum glutamate dehydrogenase (PfGDH), through both instrument-based and instrument-free approaches.  [/research/malaria-instrument-free]  Learn more

##  One-Step Selection of 'Chemical Antibody' using HAS

A totally new concept for aptamer discovery — Hydrogel for Aptamer Selection (HAS) — that selects aptamers ('chemical antibodies') in a single step in just 60 hours.  [/research/has-chemical-antibody]  Learn more

##  A Non-Invasive Wearable Stress Patch for Real-Time Cortisol Monitoring

A wearable patch based on soft microfluidics and flexible electrodes functionalized with a pseudoknot-assisted conformation-switching aptamer for pH-calibrated, continuous, non-invasive cortisol monitoring in sweat.  [/research/wearable-cortisol-patch]  Learn more

##  Continuous Monitoring of Cortisol

A reagent-free electrochemical aptasensor with a nanocomposite antifouling layer for sensitive and continuous detection of cortisol in human serum.  [/research/continuous-cortisol]  Learn more

##  EgFET Based Sensor for Malaria

An aptamer-based field effect transistor (aptaFET) biosensor using an extended gate field effect transistor with interdigitated gold microelectrodes for detection of the malaria biomarker PfGDH in serum.  [/research/egfet-malaria]  Learn more

##  Detection of SARS-CoV-2 Using a Glucometer

An aptamer-based SARS-CoV-2 salivary antigen assay employing only low-cost reagents ($3.20/test) and an off-the-shelf glucometer.  [/research/sarscov2-glucometer]  Learn more
