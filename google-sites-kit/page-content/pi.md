## Principal Investigator

Dr. Naveen Kumar Singh is an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Biomedical
Engineering (CBME), Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, where he leads the
Biosensors & Devices Lab. His research focuses on point-of-care diagnostics,
wearable and electrochemical biosensors, and programmable biomaterials for
health monitoring and disease detection.

He received his PhD from IIT Guwahati (2013–2019) and held postdoctoral
positions at the University of California San Diego (2019–2022), where he worked
with Prof. Drew Hall on advanced biosensing platforms, and was a visiting scholar
at the University of Bath with Prof. Pedro Estrela (2017). His contributions span
electrochemical sensing, aptamer-based detection, and continuous health monitoring
technologies.
